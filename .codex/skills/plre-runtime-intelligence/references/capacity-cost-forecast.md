# Capacity and Cost Forecast

## Forecast Inputs

- CPU, memory, network, storage, and queue trends.
- Workload scaling behavior.
- Node utilization and autoscaling events.
- Deployment and traffic patterns.
- Cloud cost and unit cost data.
- SLO and incident history.

## Forecast Output

- Current capacity posture.
- Short-term risks.
- Medium-term risks.
- Cost drivers.
- Optimization opportunities.
- Reliability tradeoffs.
- Recommended actions.
- Evidence gaps.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce capacity, change limits, or alter autoscaling automatically.
- Recommendations must describe expected impact and rollback path.
- Cost optimization must not weaken reliability without explicit risk acceptance.
