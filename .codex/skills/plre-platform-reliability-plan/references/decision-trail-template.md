## Entry Rules

- Record material assumptions as decisions when they affect platform direction.
- Link decisions to evidence whenever possible.
- Record rejected alternatives with rationale.
- Update status when new evidence changes the decision.
- Destructive or hard-to-reverse changes require explicit human approval before execution.
